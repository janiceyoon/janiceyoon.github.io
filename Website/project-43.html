Oceana

Branding: Business Card design, Envelope Design, Website design

2019

A website designed for a non-profit organization focused on ocean conservation, highlighting marine species and the impact of climate change. The platform allows users to explore different ocean animals while learning about the environmental challenges affecting their ecosystems.

The design follows a minimal and calming visual language, using layered blue gradients to reflect ocean depth and subtle marine illustrations to create movement and immersion. Information is presented clearly and accessibly, balancing education with a visually engaging experience. Overall, the site combines simplicity and storytelling to raise awareness and support for ocean preservation.

Tools Illustrator, Photoshop
