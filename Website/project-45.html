7 of Hearts

Graphic design

2026

In this project, I experimented with the design elements of the 7 of Hearts by focusing on texture, line, and pattern as the three primary visual traits. I explored the interplay of shape, color, repetition, and perspective to create visually dynamic compositions across each card. My mood board draws inspiration from Alice in Borderland and surreal fantasy aesthetics, incorporating a sense of motion, tension, and narrative within each design. Through this approach, I aimed to create cards that not only function visually, but also convey a deeper and more immersive storyline.

Tools Procreate, Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign
