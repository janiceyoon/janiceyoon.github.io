Photography

2026

Explores nostalgia through the lens of early 2000s girlhood, capturing fleeting moments of joy, intimacy, and memory. Inspired by the fragmented beauty of a kaleidoscope, each image reflects shifting perspectives of friendship, identity, and time.

Bright, saturated colors and playful graphic elements echo a sense of warmth and innocence. The girls become both subjects of connection, youth, and the quiet magic found in shared experiences.

Through layered compositions, light, and color, the series mimics the way memories are stored: vivid yet distorted, fragmented yet meaningful.

Photography from this series appears in Descent Magazine · Issues.
