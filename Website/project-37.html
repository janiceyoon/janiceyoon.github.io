Archive of Us Zine

Design · print

Direction

A photo-led publication that extends the Archive of Us world into print: spreads pair full-bleed imagery with quiet typographic rhythm, collage fragments, and hand-tuned color so each page reads like a memory rather than a catalog.

Layout and sequencing were built in InDesign with photography processed in Lightroom and finishing touches in Illustrator and Photoshop: balancing nostalgia, intimacy, and the tactile feel of a small-run zine.
