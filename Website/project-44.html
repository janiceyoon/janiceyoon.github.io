Presence and Absence

Design · booklet

2026

This project asks: “How do objects become archives of human presence?”

Through repetition, consistency, and systematic documentation, the work operates less as a collection of individual photographs and more as a cohesive visual system. Uniformity establishes structure and anonymity, while organic clustering preserves the emotional quality of accumulation, memory, and lived experience.

Each image is visually distinct, representing different forms of absence · temporary, permanent, intimate, or forgotten.

This project explores the relationship between absence and presence through the quiet evidence embedded in everyday objects. Rather than depicting the human body directly, I focus on the traces it leaves behind: wear, stains, fading, distortion, and adaptation. These marks become proof of touch, time, and lived experience.

Tools InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator
