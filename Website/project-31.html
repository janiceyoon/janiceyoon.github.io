ktown

Installation

2024

This installation explores my Korean American identity through the landscape of Koreatown, representing the experience of growing up between two cultures. The composition is physically divided into contrasting halves, symbolizing the duality of language, culture, and belonging.

Tools Felt, newspaper, fiberfill, rhinestones, cut paper, mixed media
