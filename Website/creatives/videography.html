2023 · Installation

Joseph's doodles

Installation, motion, videography

Industry
Art & installation
Type
Long-form documentation

An installation dedicated to my younger brother, who has autism and finds joy in doodling and watching farm animals. I recreated his drawings as a miniature, three-dimensional playground · keeping their original forms to preserve his perspective · so viewers can step into a world where repetition is calming, movement is exciting, and small moments hold meaning.
