Art & Design, USC · Marketing minor

Hi! I’m Janice, a multidisciplinary creative and visual designer. I’m currently a sophomore at USC, studying Art & Design with a minor in Marketing.

Growing up, art has always been my safe space—a place to process, explore, and simply be. This personal website is an extension of that: a welcomed space where creativity lives freely, stories are told honestly, and connection is at the center of everything.

Here, I explore self-expression, emotion, and the quiet beauty of human connection, translating those feelings into work that spans design, photography, and fine arts. My greatest strength is my versatility, weaving multiple mediums together to craft visual narratives that feel whole, intentional, and deeply felt.
